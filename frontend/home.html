Six ways Neutrix keeps recovery safe

Our responsible AI framework ensures patients and family caregivers receive safe, non-diagnostic guidance at all times.

Grounded in User Data & Verified Guidelines

01. Strict Non-Diagnostic Policy −

Neutrix will never diagnose medical conditions, prescribe new drugs, or suggest altering doctor-prescribed treatment plans.

02. Grounded AI Explanations +

Every generated summary displays exact source citations from the uploaded hospital discharge summary or prescription.

03. Immediate Emergency Escalation +

High-risk symptoms like chest pain or difficulty breathing immediately trigger recommendations to seek emergency medical help.

04. Malayalam & English Support +

Bilingual translation allows family members to view instructions in their preferred local language.

05. Doctor Consultation Brief Generator +

Summarizes recent recovery vitals, symptoms, and missed doses into a single concise note for your next doctor visit.

06. Complete Data Privacy +

Uploaded clinical records are kept private, local, and never shared or sold to third parties.
