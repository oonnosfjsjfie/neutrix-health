Six ways Neutrix keeps recovery safe

Our responsible AI framework ensures patients and family caregivers receive safe, non-diagnostic guidance at all times.

Grounded in your own reports

01. Strict Non-Diagnostic Policy −

Neutrix will never diagnose medical conditions, prescribe new drugs, or suggest altering doctor-prescribed treatment plans.

02. Grounded AI Explanations +

The AI is instructed to use only what is written in your uploaded reports and to quote values, doses and dates exactly. It never adds medicines or results. Exercises show the exact line from your report they came from.

03. Immediate Emergency Escalation +

Danger signs like chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting or severe bleeding in the Recovery Tracker show an urgent warning to contact a doctor or go to the nearest hospital. The progress report flags them for the doctor too.

04. Malayalam & English Support +

Bilingual translation allows family members to view instructions in their preferred local language.

05. Doctor Brief & Progress Report +

Doctor Brief emails a clear update to your doctor. The Overall Progress Report turns your medicine ticks, readings, symptoms and exercise into one report for the next visit.

06. Your Data & Privacy +

Your data is saved to your own account. Only you can open your medical records, on any device you sign in with. Reports are read by Google Gemini to make the summaries. The Neutrix team can see only your email and phone number (to contact you) and any feedback you send, never your profile or medical records. Nothing is sold. Exercise camera video stays on your device.
